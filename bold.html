Our name & our roots

What is al-Ma'un?

Our name comes from Surah Al-Ma'un — the 107th chapter of the Qur'an — where faith is measured not by grand gestures but by the small kindnesses we show a neighbor: a meal, a coat, a hand held out. That is the whole idea. No bureaucracy. No red tape. Just neighbors, refusing to look away.

We are the humanitarian arm of Masjid As-Sabur, the oldest mosque in Las Vegas, rooted in the Historic Westside since 2003. Our volunteers earned national recognition for Hurricane Katrina relief, and were once honored by a visit from Muhammad Ali. But the real work is quieter and constant — showing up, week after week, for the block we call home.
