How it all works together

One neighbor, the whole circle.

Real life doesn't come in tidy boxes. The family that walks in for groceries might also need the clinic; the person coming home from prison needs a bed, a sponsor, and a warm meal all at once. So we treat these six programs as one set of hands, not six departments — wraparound care that meets the whole person, offered with dignity, and never rationed by paperwork or belief. You come as you are. We take it from there.

Program 01 · Food & Nutrition

Nobody goes to bed hungry.

The pantry on Morgan Avenue is open to anyone who's short this week — no ID, no means test, no lecture. We hand out groceries, cook hot meals, and deliver to folks who can't get out. Some of the produce is grown right here in our own community garden, so the food that reaches your table is food we'd serve our own family.

Who it serves

Families stretching the last days of the month, homebound seniors, folks between paychecks, and anyone who walks up hungry — Muslim or not.

What we offer

  • Food pantry — groceries to take home, no questions asked
  • Hot meals cooked and served on-site
  • Grocery delivery for homebound seniors and neighbors
  • A community garden and farmers market with fresh produce
  • The Nutrition Passport — learning to cook and eat well

How to get help: Walk in at 711 Morgan Ave during open hours, or call (702) 647-2500.

Program 02 · Free Health Clinic

Care for those with nowhere to turn.

We built a free health clinic hand-in-hand with UNLV so a neighbor without insurance still has somewhere to go. Volunteer clinicians handle the everyday things that get dangerous when ignored — a check-up, a wound that won't heal, an eye exam, help affording a prescription.

Who it serves

Uninsured and underinsured neighbors, seniors, returning citizens, and anyone the healthcare system has quietly left behind.

What we offer

  • General check-ups and health screenings
  • Wound care and follow-up
  • Eye exams and vision help
  • Prescription assistance

How to get help: Walk in at 711 Morgan Ave during clinic hours, or call (702) 647-2500 to ask when the clinic is open.

Program 03 · Education & Workshops

Knowledge that protects & heals.

Some of the deepest needs on the block aren't material — they're a person carrying grief with no one to carry it with, or a family who doesn't know their rights until it's too late. Our workshops meet those needs plainly, led by neighbors and trusted professionals, including the classes we run through Nisaa's Academy.

Who it serves

Neighbors healing from trauma or loss, families who need to understand their legal rights, and lifelong learners of every age.

What we offer

  • Trauma-informed support groups
  • Know-your-rights and legal workshops
  • Grief and bereavement support
  • Classes and mentoring through Nisaa's Academy

How to get help: Walk in at 711 Morgan Ave during open hours, or call (702) 647-2500 for the next workshop schedule.

Program 04 · Stabilization & Reentry

A second chance is sacred.

When someone comes home from incarceration, gets clean, or is one late bill from losing their home, the first ninety days decide everything. We walk alongside people through that stretch — our CHANCES reentry program, Millati Islami recovery support, and direct help with rent and utilities so a hard month doesn't become a lost home.

Who it serves

Returning citizens rebuilding after incarceration, neighbors in recovery, and families facing eviction or a shut-off notice.

What we offer

  • CHANCES reentry support for returning citizens
  • Millati Islami recovery and sobriety fellowship
  • Emergency rent assistance
  • Utility assistance to keep the lights and water on

How to get help: Walk in at 711 Morgan Ave during open hours, or call (702) 647-2500 to speak with someone.

Program 05 · Youth Development

The next generation, believed in.

The surest way to change a block is to invest in the kids growing up on it. We pair young people with mentors, run a chess club that teaches patience and strategy, host real talks about the things teens actually face, and sponsor youth who need a hand to reach the next thing.

Who it serves

Kids and teens across the Historic Westside — and the parents raising them.

What we offer

  • One-on-one mentorship
  • Chess club — focus, patience, and strategy
  • Youth talks on real-life challenges
  • Sponsorship for youth activities and opportunities

How to get help: Walk in at 711 Morgan Ave during open hours, or call (702) 647-2500 to get a young person connected.

Program 06 · Community & Culture

The glue of a neighborhood.

A neighborhood is more than its needs — it's its people, its art, its shared table. We host cultural and interfaith gatherings that bring folks together across every line, and we organize cleanups that let neighbors take visible pride in the block they call home.

Who it serves

Everyone on the Historic Westside — and every guest, of every faith, who wants to be a good neighbor.

What we offer

  • Arts and culture events
  • Interfaith gatherings and dialogue
  • Neighborhood cleanups
  • Community celebrations like Humanitarian Day

How to get help: Walk in at 711 Morgan Ave during open hours, or call (702) 647-2500 to join the next gathering.
